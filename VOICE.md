# How ParentVeda speaks

Read this before writing any copy for the site. The site already looks right;
this is what makes it *read* right.

## The tagline

**Your trusted parenting companion.** The official tagline (2026-10-01): the
home hero line, page titles, link previews, the footer and the structured data
all read it from `site.tagline` in `lib/site.ts`. Use it word for word.

## The idea, in one line

**Your child's story starts with you.**

A child's life begins long before birth. It begins with the two people who
decide they want one, and in every choice they make after that. ParentVeda
exists to show them the best they can do for their child at each step, and to
stay beside them while they do it.

## The four phases, told from the child's side

| Phase | What it is for the parents | What it means for the child |
|---|---|---|
| Trying to conceive | Exciting, and often hard | Becoming the best place for a baby to begin: body, mind and the two of you |
| Pregnancy | When it gets real, and everything changes | What you eat, feel and live around, your baby shares |
| Parenting | Guiding someone, often without knowing what's right | Becoming the parent your child needs, one day at a time |
| Skilling | Wanting them ready, without turning childhood into school | Skills for the world they will actually grow up in |

## Who is speaking

A warm, well-read elder who has seen it all before and is on your side. Not
a salesperson and not a textbook. Someone you could call at 2 a.m.

## Every section moves in three beats

1. **I understand.** Name what this moment feels like, in their words.
2. **What matters for your child now.** The practice, and why it's worth doing.
3. **How we walk with you.** The features, as proof, never as the pitch.

A section that opens with a feature list has skipped the first two beats.

## The one rule that keeps it honest

**We sell a way of life, never an outcome.**

We are confident that good food, calm, rest, attention and practice are worth
doing. We never promise what they will produce.

| Say | Never say |
|---|---|
| "the best start you can give" | "a smarter baby", "higher IQ" |
| "worth doing", "it helps", "here's why" | "boosts", "guarantees", "proven to" |
| "gives them room to grow" | anything about a child being slow, dumb, behind |
| population facts, when they ease pressure | "your chance", percentages, success rates |
| "your doctor decides" | anything that sounds like a diagnosis |
| "when you're ready" | fear, urgency, "or else" |

Walnuts are good food; they do not make an Einstein. We can say the first and
must never imply the second. Indian advertising standards (ASCI) expect any
health claim to be backed by evidence, and the app itself refuses personalised
odds. The site keeps the same line.

## Every section holds together

The eyebrow names the topic, the headline makes one claim, and everything
under it is evidence for that claim. If the eyebrow says "How it helps", the
headline says how it helps and the cards show it helping. Three layers saying
three different things is the fastest way to lose a careful reader.

## Features arrive as moments, not labels

| Menu card | ParentVeda |
|---|---|
| "Kick counter" | "Count kicks after dinner, and know what's normal for your baby." |
| "33 symptoms" | "Tap where it feels different. Most things are ordinary, and we say so. Five are not, and we say that faster." |
| "Scans & tests door" | "“What is this scan actually looking for?” Nine scans, explained before the day." |

## Guides, not doors

In the app a topic is a "door". On the site it is a **guide**, because a visitor
has never heard the word door. Each guide opens with the question a parent is
actually asking, then why it matters for their child, then what is inside.

## Skilling

Maths is a brain gym, not marks. Coding is the language we use to talk to AI.
Speaking, expression and feelings are life skills. Effort grows ability; we say
that in our own words and never label a child.

## Before publishing a line, check

- Does it start from how they feel, or from what we have?
- Is the child the reason, somewhere in it?
- Could it be read as a promise of an outcome? Rewrite it.
- Would it make an anxious reader more anxious? Rewrite it.
- Is every number real, and every feature built?
