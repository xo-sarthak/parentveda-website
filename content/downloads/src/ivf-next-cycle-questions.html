Questions to ask before your next IVF cycle

A cycle that does not work is hard, and it is not your fault. Before you decide on the next step, ask for a review meeting with your doctor. These questions help you leave that meeting knowing what happened and what will change. Tick the ones you want to ask. You do not need to ask them all.

Understanding the last cycle

  • How did my body respond to the injections? Was it what you expected?
  • How many eggs were collected, and how many were mature?
  • How many fertilised, and how many grew into embryos?
  • On which day were embryos transferred or frozen, and what were their grades?
  • How did my uterine lining look at transfer?
  • Did anything happen in this cycle that you would do differently?
  • Can I have a written summary of this cycle for my records?

Tests to talk about

  • Should we recheck thyroid, sugar, prolactin or vitamin D?
  • Does my uterus need a closer look, such as a hysteroscopy?
  • Should my husband repeat the semen test, or have other tests?
  • Is genetic testing of embryos useful for us, or not? Why?
  • Are there other tests you suggest, and what would each one change?
  • Which tests do you not think we need, and why?

What might change next time

  • Will you change the medicine plan or the doses?
  • Should we use a frozen embryo transfer instead of a fresh one, or the other way round?
  • How many embryos do you suggest transferring, and why?
  • Would ICSI or any lab method help in our case?
  • Is there anything in our food, weight, sleep or habits worth working on first?
  • How long should we wait before starting again?

Practical questions

  • If we have frozen embryos, what is the plan for them?
  • What will the next cycle cost? What is included, and what is extra?
  • How many visits and injections will it need? Can some be done near home?
  • At what point would you suggest a different approach?

For the two of you

  • Does the clinic offer counselling, or can you suggest someone?
  • Is it all right for us to take a break before deciding?

Before the meeting

Bring your cycle papers, scan reports and medicine list. Write your three most important questions at the top. Go together if you can, and ask if you can note down or record the answers. It is fine to ask the doctor to explain something again.
