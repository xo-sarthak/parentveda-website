Blood pressure log sheet

Checking your BP at home gives your doctor a clearer picture than one reading at the clinic. Write each reading here and take this sheet to every visit. One high number does not mean something is wrong, and one normal number does not mean you can skip a dose. Your doctor reads the pattern.

How to take a good reading

  • No tea, coffee, smoking or exercise for 30 minutes before.
  • Empty your bladder. Sit quietly for 5 minutes.
  • Sit with your back supported, feet flat on the floor, legs uncrossed.
  • Cuff on your bare upper arm. Rest that arm on a table at heart level.
  • Do not talk while the machine works.
  • Take 2 readings, 1 minute apart. Write both, or as your doctor asks.
  • Use the same arm and a similar time of day each time.

Go to hospital now if

  • Your reading is at or above the level your doctor told you to act on.
  • You have a bad headache that does not go away.
  • Your vision blurs, or you see flashing lights or spots.
  • You have pain high in the belly, under the ribs, especially on the right.
  • Your face, hands or feet swell suddenly.
  • You feel breathless, or have a fit.
  • Your baby is moving less than usual, or you have bleeding.
